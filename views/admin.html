Team & crew stores

Each store gets its own link. Everyone orders their own size and pays for their own gear. When the store closes you get one print sheet and a packing list by name. Stores close on their own at the end of the closing date and you get an email.

Package deals

New package

A package is a fixed deal, like "100 tees for $399". Customers pick a color, split the sizes to add up to the package size, upload a logo and add it to their bag.
